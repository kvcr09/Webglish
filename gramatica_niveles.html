Evaluación de Gramática

Selecciona el nivel que deseas evaluar (10 preguntas aleatorias por intento)

Nivel A1 - Principiante

Temas: Verb to be, Present Simple, Pronouns, Articles (a/an/the), Basic Prepositions.

Nivel A2 - Elemental

Temas: Past Simple, Present Continuous, Comparatives & Superlatives, Going to / Will.

Nivel B1 - Intermedio

Temas: Present Perfect, First & Second Conditionals, Modal Verbs, Passive Voice (básica).

Nivel B2 - Intermedio Alto

Temas: Past Perfect, Third Conditional, Reported Speech, Relative Clauses, Passive Voice avanzada.

Nivel C1 - Avanzado

Temas: Mixed Conditionals, Inversion, Subjunctive Mood, Advanced Modals, Participle Clauses.

Nivel C2 - Maestría

Temas: Cleft Sentences, Ellipsis & Substitution, Nuanced Phrasal Verbs, Formal Subjunctive.
